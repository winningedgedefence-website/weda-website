Refund Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026 The Winning Edge Defence Academy, Dehradun

We would rather you knew this before you paid than discovered it afterwards. Our fees are non-refundable. Please read the one page.

The Short Version

  • The ₹1,000 one-week demo is non-refundable. It buys a week of real teaching, and that week is delivered.
  • Course fees are non-refundable once a seat is confirmed — whether the student attends or not.
  • We do return money in three situations: a duplicate or failed payment, a batch we cancel before it starts, and an amount we have charged in error.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains it, because a rule you understand is one you are less likely to be upset by.

Why We Work This Way

When a seat is allotted, we stop offering it to anyone else. Faculty are engaged for the session, the batch size is set, and material is printed against the names on the roll. A student who withdraws in week six does not free up a seat that another family can still use, and the cost of holding it has already been spent.

This is also why the demo exists. We would rather you spent ₹1,000 finding out whether we suit your child than ₹45,000 discovering it in October. Use the week properly — sit in the classes, meet the faculty, ask us hard questions.

1. The One-Week Demo — ₹1,000

A registration fee of ₹1,000 gives a student seven days of attendance in a live batch, with the same faculty, classes and material as an enrolled student.

  • It is non-refundable in every case. Not if you decide against enrolling, not if the student attends only part of the week, not if the student does not turn up at all.
  • It is not adjusted against course fees unless a counsellor has confirmed that to you in writing.
  • It carries no obligation to enrol. That is the point of it.

Please book the demo week only when you know the student can attend it. Once booked, treat the ₹1,000 as spent.

2. Course Fees

Course fees are non-refundable once a seat is confirmed. A seat is confirmed when we receive the fee.

In particular, we do not refund — in whole or in part — where:

  • The student withdraws, at any stage, for any reason.
  • The student stops attending, or attends irregularly.
  • The family relocates, or the student’s school timetable changes.
  • The student is not selected in the examination they were preparing for.
  • The student is removed from the course for misconduct under clause 6 of our Terms & Conditions.
  • The student’s eligibility fails on age, category, medical grounds or document verification — matters decided by the examining authority, not by us.

Unpaid instalments remain payable for the session enrolled.

3. Where We Do Refund

Three situations, and we do not make you argue for them.

A duplicate or failed payment

If you are charged twice for the same fee, or money leaves your account for a transaction that did not complete, the extra amount is returned in full. It was never ours. Send us the transaction reference and we will trace it.

A batch we cancel before it begins

If we cancel a batch outright before its first class — too few enrolments, or a reason of our own — you may take a full refund of the course fee, or move to another batch, whichever you prefer. We will offer you both.

This does not apply to a change of timing, a change of faculty, a batch moved online, or a rescheduled class. Those are covered in clause 5 of the Terms & Conditions, and in each case we still deliver the teaching hours you paid for.

An amount we charged in error

If we have billed you for the wrong course, the wrong duration or the wrong amount, we correct it and return the difference. Tell us as soon as you spot it.

4. Transfers Instead Of Refunds

A refund is not the only thing we can do, and often it is not the most useful. At our discretion, and on a written request from the parent, we may:

  • Move a student to a different batch of the same course — a later start, or a different timing.
  • Move a student between modes — classroom to online, or the reverse — with any difference in fee adjusted either way.
  • Hold a paid fee against the next session where a genuine medical or family reason prevents a student from continuing.

These are concessions, not entitlements, and we decide them case by case. But if something serious has happened to your family, come and talk to us before you write the fee off. We have made arrangements before.

5. How To Raise A Request

Write to us — email or a letter at the counter, not a verbal request, so there is a record on both sides.

  • Email — winningedgedefence@gmail.com, with “Refund” in the subject line.
  • Include the student’s name, the course and batch, the receipt number or transaction reference, the amount, and what you are asking for.
  • We acknowledge within three working days and give you a decision within fifteen working days.
  • Approved refunds are paid within twenty-one working days of approval, to the same account the payment came from. We do not pay refunds in cash or to a third party’s account.

Bank charges or payment-gateway fees already deducted by the provider are not ours to return.

6. If You Disagree With Our Decision

Ask for it to be reviewed by the Director. Say so in writing and it will be looked at again by someone who did not take the first decision.

Nothing on this page takes away a right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or any other applicable law. Any dispute is subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at Dehradun, Uttarakhand, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

7. Changes To This Policy

We may revise this page. The version in force on the date you paid is the one that applies to that payment — a later change does not reach backwards.
