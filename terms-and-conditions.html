Terms & Conditions

1. What These Terms Cover

These terms apply to this website, thewinningedge.co.in, and to every course we run — classroom at our Dehradun centre, live online classes, and recorded or digital material.

By enrolling a student, paying a fee, attending a class or using this website, you accept these terms. They form the agreement between you and The Winning Edge Defence Academy, WEDA Tower, Kailash Vihar, Sunderwala, Dehradun 248008, Uttarakhand, India.

2. Who Can Enrol

Most of our students are minors. A student under eighteen must be enrolled by a parent or legal guardian, who accepts these terms on the student’s behalf and is responsible for the fees.

You confirm that the information you give us at admission — the student’s name, date of birth, class, school and category — is true. If it turns out to be wrong in a way that affects the student’s eligibility for the examination they are preparing for, that is not something we can undo for you, and the fee position is as set out in our Refund Policy.

3. The One-Week Demo

We offer a one-week demo at a registration fee of ₹1,000 so a family can see our teaching before committing to a full course.

  • What it buys. Seven days of attendance in the live batch, with the same faculty, the same classes and the same material as an enrolled student.
  • It is non-refundable. The fee is charged for a week of teaching that is actually delivered, so it is not returned whether or not you go on to enrol. Please treat ₹1,000 as spent at the moment you book it.
  • It carries no obligation. At the end of the week you are free to enrol or to walk away, and nobody at the academy will make that awkward.
  • It is not adjusted against course fees unless a counsellor has confirmed otherwise to you in writing.

The full position on fees and refunds is on the Refund Policy page, and it is short.

4. Fees

  • The rates on our Fee Structure page apply to the session stated there. Fees for a later session may differ.
  • GST is included in the fees shown. We do not add tax at the counter.
  • A seat is confirmed when the fee is received — not when it is promised. Batches fill, and we allocate in the order payments clear.
  • Instalments, where agreed, must be paid on the dates agreed. Continued attendance may be suspended if an instalment is overdue.
  • Always take a receipt. Pay only into the academy’s own account or at our counter, and insist on a receipt in the academy’s name. We are not answerable for money handed to any individual without one.

5. Batches, Timings And Changes

Start dates, timings and the mode of a batch are announced on our Batches page and by our counselling team.

We may need to change a class timing, move a class online, substitute a faculty member, merge a batch with low enrolment, or reschedule for a public holiday, examination date or circumstances outside our control. We will give you as much notice as we have and we will deliver the teaching hours you paid for, even if the calendar has to shift to do it.

If we cancel a batch outright before it begins, see the Refund Policy — that situation is refundable.

6. Attendance And Conduct

We are a defence-preparation academy and we hold students to the standard the services would. We expect punctuality, civility to faculty and fellow students, honesty in tests, and care of the premises.

We may ask a student to leave the course, without a refund, for:

  • Cheating in an internal test or mock examination.
  • Violence, bullying, harassment or intimidation of any student or member of staff.
  • Possession or use of alcohol, tobacco or any prohibited substance on our premises.
  • Wilful damage to the premises, equipment or property of others.
  • Sharing, recording, reselling or republishing our study material or classes — see clause 8.
  • Conduct that makes the classroom unworkable for the other students in it, after a warning to the parent.

Except where the conduct is serious enough to act on at once, we will speak to the parent before we take a student off a course.

7. Online Classes

  • Your login is for one student. Do not share it, and do not let anyone else attend in the student’s place. Shared credentials may be disabled without refund.
  • Do not record, screen-capture, stream or redistribute a live class. Our faculty are entitled to teach without being published.
  • You provide your own device and internet connection. We cannot credit back a class missed because of a power cut, a weak connection or a device failure at your end — though we will help you catch up where we can.
  • Recordings, where provided, are for the enrolled student’s revision, for the stated access period only.

8. Study Material And Intellectual Property

Our notes, question banks, mock papers, recorded lectures, worksheets, videos, the WEDA name and logo, and the text and design of this website are our property or are licensed to us.

Material issued to an enrolled student is for that student’s own preparation. You may not copy it for others, upload it to a sharing site or messaging group, sell it, or use it to teach or coach anyone else — whether for money or not.

This is not a formality we have borrowed from another website. Material of ours circulating freely devalues what every paying parent has bought, and we do act on it.

9. What We Promise, And What We Do Not

This is the most important clause on the page, so we will not bury it.

We promise to teach. Qualified faculty, the hours you paid for, honest assessment of where your child stands, and a straight answer when you ask how they are doing.

We do not promise selection. No coaching institute can, and any institute that tells you otherwise is not being honest with you. Admission to a Sainik School, Rashtriya Military School, RIMC, the NDA or any other institution is decided by that institution and its selecting authority, on its own criteria — written marks, medical fitness, document verification, availability of seats in a category, and in some cases an interview. None of that is in our hands.

Results shown on our Results page are the genuine achievements of past students. They are a record, not a forecast. A student’s outcome depends above all on their own work.

10. We Are Not Affiliated With Any Government Body

The Winning Edge Defence Academy is a private coaching institute. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, appointed by or acting for the Ministry of Defence, the Sainik Schools Society, any Rashtriya Military School, the Rashtriya Indian Military College, the National Defence Academy, the UPSC, the NTA or any other government department or examining body.

We prepare students for their examinations. We have no role in conducting them, no influence over any result, and no ability to secure a seat for anyone. Names of examinations and institutions appear on this site only to describe what we coach for.

Always confirm notifications, eligibility, dates and syllabus on the official website of the conducting body. We summarise this information carefully, but the official notification governs — and it changes.

11. Photographs And Publicity

We photograph and film at the academy and publish some of it on this website and our social channels. By enrolling you agree to this, and we do not pay for its use.

You may opt out at any time, in writing, with no consequence for your child’s teaching, and we will remove existing images from anything we control if you ask. How we handle this is set out on the Privacy Policy page.

We keep the content here accurate and current, and we correct mistakes when we find them. We cannot warrant that every page is free of error or always up to date, particularly where it describes an external examination whose rules the conducting body may change.

This site links to third-party sites — YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps and official examination portals. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices.

Do not attempt to break into, overload, scrape wholesale or interfere with this website.

We are not liable for a student’s examination result, for a loss of opportunity arising from a result, for a decision taken by an examining authority, or for any indirect or consequential loss. Nothing here limits a liability that cannot lawfully be limited, and nothing here takes away a right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

These terms are governed by the laws of India. The courts at Dehradun, Uttarakhand have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.

Before it reaches that point, please talk to us. Nearly everything we have ever been asked to fix was fixable in a phone call.
